### 4. Methodology - the GreyRadius TEV framework (8 modules; mirrors actual report structures)
1. Project rationale and promoter assessment
2. Market and demand analysis - primary research, demand-supply gap, target customer segments, competitive landscape
3. Business model analysis - current operations, revenue model, product mix
4. Technical assessment - site and land, plant layout, process technology, vendor evaluation and site visits, installed capacity and throughput estimation, utilities, manpower, approvals
5. Project cost - civil works, plant and machinery, misc fixed assets, preliminary/preoperative, IDC, working capital margin
6. Means of finance and bankability - debt-equity structure, DSCR, lender covenants
7. Financial analysis - projections, capacity utilisation ramp, sensitivity analysis, break-even
8. Risk assessment, mitigation and the go/no-go recommendation

### 5. Process timeline (6 stages, weeks 1-8 typical)
Scoping and data room (wk 1) - Site visit and technical diligence (wk 2-3) - Primary market research (wk 2-4, parallel) - Technical and cost assessment (wk 4-5) - Financial model and sensitivities (wk 5-6) - Draft for lender/board review, final report and sanction support (wk 7-8)

### 8. Challenges we solve (challenge - how we overcome it)
- Promoter/vendor data gaps and optimistic quotes -> independent market triangulation of P&M and civil costs; best-estimate methodology documented for lenders
- Cost overrun and IDC escalation mid-project -> re-appraisal TEV quantifying overrun causes; revised means of finance and additional-facility case the lender can sanction
- Technology vendor overclaim -> site visits, reference plant checks, throughput validation against nameplate
- Offtake uncertainty -> distributor and offtake scouting with LOIs before financial close; feedstock/supply agreements assessed for bankability
- Lender scrutiny and format rejection -> report structures built to bank/DFI appraisal formats; sanction-cycle support included
- Approvals and municipal compliance drift -> dual site-plan discipline (economic vs municipality), approvals register with owner and dates in the implementation schedule
