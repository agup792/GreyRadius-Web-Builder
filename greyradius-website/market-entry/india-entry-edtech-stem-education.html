Market Entry - India

India entry for education and STEM companies - two doors, opened together

Education products enter India through two doors at once: the distributor who reaches schools, and the regulator who certifies hardware. Companies that queue at one door while ignoring the other lose the academic year. Our playbook holds both open - with the school-channel field mapping to prove where demand actually sits.

250M+ school students - the world's largest K-12 system 600+ institutions mapped in one field programme BIS toy and electronics certification handled in parallel

Who this is for

STEM, robotics and maker-education companies

Hardware plus curriculum needs school-channel distributors and BIS certification - two workstreams, one calendar, usually discovered in the wrong order.

Education content and edtech platforms

Institutional sales into India's school and higher-ed systems run on relationships and evidence - both mappable, neither improvisable.

Educational products and learning materials brands

From clay to coding kits: the school supply chain has structure, and entering it deliberately beats distributor roulette.

Context

Why India, why now - for education companies

India's National Education Policy pushes experiential, skill-based and coding-inclusive learning - creating institutional budgets for exactly the products STEM and maker-education companies sell.

The K-12 system is the world's largest and structurally under-equipped in hands-on learning tools - private school chains and progressive state programmes are both buying, on different cycles.

BIS certification requirements for toys and electronic learning products reshaped the import landscape - compliant entrants now face less grey-market competition than before the rules tightened, a quiet advantage for companies that do certification properly.

Process

The India entry framework - six phases, every one ends in a named deliverable

For education mandates, phase 2 includes institution-level field mapping - schools scored by curriculum, budget authority and buying context - so the distributor search in phase 5 targets partners who reach the institutions that matter.

  • 1

    Business assessment

    Working sessions inside your business: portfolio, pricing, capacity, export experience and India ambitions - so the entry plan fits the company you actually are. Deliverable: assessment memo and scope lock.

  • 2

    Opportunity sizing and validation

    Institution-level field mapping: schools scored by curriculum alignment, budget authority and buying context - not database synthesis. Category demand and price-tier fit validated through buyer interviews. Deliverable: opportunity report with institution heatmap and Go / No-Go recommendation.

  • 3

    Competitive and regulatory intelligence

    BIS certification applicability and filing plan initiated, competitor route-to-market mapping and school supply chain margin benchmarks. Deliverable: competitive map, regulatory register and certification filing plan.

  • 4

    GTM and commercial design

    Channel architecture across private schools, chains and B2G pathways, academic-year launch calendar working backwards from target season, and revenue projection framework. Deliverable: entry roadmap with commercial model.

  • 5

    Distributor search and appointment

    200-300 school-channel candidates longlisted from field networks, scored to a 20-30 shortlist on school-cluster reach and service capability, then negotiated to 2-3 signed partnerships. Deliverable: signed distribution agreements.

  • 6

    Launch and governance

    Launch calendar, sales cadence, partner scorecards and review governance for the first 90 days - expansion gated on milestones. Deliverable: operating launch plan and governance system.

Regulatory

BIS certification - the hardware door

Toys and many electronic learning products fall under BIS mandatory certification, with factory audits for foreign manufacturers and additional scrutiny for Chinese-origin components in products assembled elsewhere. Certification timelines run months and belong at the start of the entry calendar - filed in parallel with channel work, coordinated through specialist partners, so the academic-year launch window is set by strategy rather than by the queue at the regulator.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do we sell education products to schools in India?

Through the school supply chain's real structure: distributors and dealers who serve school clusters, direct institutional sales for chains and premium schools, and state programmes for scale. Which mix fits depends on price point and curriculum linkage - our field mapping scores the institutions before the channel gets designed.

Is BIS certification required for educational toys and STEM kits in India?

Toys under 14 years fall under mandatory BIS certification, and many electronic learning products are covered under electronics registration schemes - including foreign-manufacturer factory audit requirements. File early, hero products first; the certification calendar and the academic calendar must be reconciled, not discovered.

Should we target private schools or government programmes first?

Private and chain schools buy faster and anchor references; government programmes deliver scale on long cycles with delivery-framework procurement. The proven sequence for most entrants: private-school beachhead funding a prepared B2G pursuit - our state-programme delivery experience covers the second door.

When should we launch to match the Indian academic year?

School purchasing concentrates ahead of the April session start, with a secondary window mid-year. Working backwards - certification, distributor appointment, dealer stocking - a serious entry decision needs 9-12 months of runway before the target season.

Can you help with government education tenders in India?

Yes - programme mapping, bid/no-bid discipline, consortium design and proposal-grade delivery frameworks with phases and quality gates. Our B2G education work includes state-scale programme frameworks; the door is different from private channels and the proposal must be too.
