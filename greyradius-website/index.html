FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does GreyRadius Consulting do?

GreyRadius is a boutique strategy and growth consulting firm with offices across South Asia, the Gulf, Southeast Asia, and Europe. We help founders, CXOs, and business leaders with opportunity assessment, market entry, GTM execution, feasibility studies, pitch books, and fundraising – grounded in primary research, AI-enabled for speed, and validated by our experts before every deliverable.

How is GreyRadius different from a large consulting firm?

Every engagement is run by the senior partner who sold it. We ground every insight in primary research – real interviews with buyers, experts, and channel partners, not database downloads. AI accelerates the analytical work; our partners review every finding against primary sources before it becomes a recommendation. We bill on outputs, not hours.

What markets does GreyRadius serve?

We work with clients expanding into the Middle East, Southeast Asia, South Asia, and Europe. Our teams are present in four markets with active mandates across more than twenty countries.

How does GreyRadius use AI in consulting delivery?

We use AI internally across research synthesis, financial modelling, competitive mapping, and outreach automation. This compresses delivery timelines by up to 3x without replacing the primary research and senior judgment that drive real decisions.

What is primary research in consulting?

Primary research means gathering insight directly from the market – through structured interviews, expert panels, CATI surveys, and field research. GreyRadius conducts ~30+ interviews and 200+ surveys per mandate. This is the input that desk research and AI cannot replicate.

How can a consulting firm help businesses enter new markets successfully?

Structured market analysis, competitor benchmarking and entry strategy - then execution. GreyRadius runs market entry end to end: prioritisation, entry mode, partnerships, regulatory pathway and first-customer acquisition across India, the Middle East, Southeast Asia and Africa.

What is a go-to-market (GTM) strategy and why does it matter?

A GTM strategy defines how you position, price, reach customers and build repeatable sales. It matters because most growth stalls are execution stalls: GreyRadius designs the GTM plan and runs the first 90 days of the motion.

What is an opportunity assessment and when do you need one?

A structured evaluation of market size, demand, competition and viability before committing capital - grounded in 30+ expert interviews per mandate. Commission one before any material market, product or expansion bet.

How does GreyRadius support a new business launch or geographic expansion?

Business model design, demand validation, financial and operational planning, then hands-on execution - entity, partners, channels, first revenue. One team from decision to delivery.

How can companies validate product-market fit before launching?

Customer research, demand testing, competitor benchmarking and pricing analysis - primary evidence, not desk research. We tell you what customers will pay for before you build the launch around it.

How does a consulting firm support fundraising and valuation?

Financial models, valuation analysis grounded in market benchmarks, investor-ready pitch materials and targeted introductions. GreyRadius pitchbooks are built on investor interviews, which is why they close faster.
