Gaming and esports consulting in India

India is the world's largest mobile gaming market by downloads and among the fastest growing by revenue, yet it remains the market where global publishers most consistently misread economics. Average revenue per user is a fraction of developed markets, real-money gaming has been through a regulatory and tax upheaval, and distribution is contested between global app stores, local platforms and creator ecosystems. At the same time, India supplies the world's game development and art production talent. GreyRadius helps publishers, platforms, esports ventures and investors size what is truly monetisable, structure India entries and build the local partnerships that decide outcomes.

Market Reality

What makes this market hard.

  • Download leadership does not convert to revenue leadership: India's paying-user ratios and ARPU remain low. Success requires India-specific monetisation design - pricing ladders, ad-monetisation balance and live-ops localisation - not imported playbooks.
  • The regulatory ground is still moving: GST treatment of online gaming, state-level RMG bans and evolving central rules have restructured the sector. Any India strategy needs regulatory scenario planning built in.
  • Distribution and discovery are contested: App store policies, alternative distribution, and creator-led discovery loops shape user acquisition costs. Publishers relying on global UA playbooks overpay for low-retention installs.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is India's gaming market actually monetisable?+

Yes, selectively. Revenue crossed USD 3 billion with in-app purchase behaviour improving on UPI rails, but ARPU remains far below developed markets. Winning models are designed for Indian price points and ad-monetisation balance - we benchmark yours against local leaders before entry.

What did the 28% GST decision change?+

It applied 28% GST on deposits for online money gaming, compressing RMG margins and triggering consolidation and litigation. Skill-gaming models are being restructured around it. Any RMG-adjacent strategy needs scenario planning across pending rulings - which we build into every assessment.

Do we need a local publishing partner?+

Not always, but local live-ops, creator networks and payment optimisation are hard to run remotely. We screen publishing, agency and hybrid structures against your genre and margin targets.

Is Indian esports investable after the title bans?+

The ecosystem reset around publisher risk taught hard lessons. Viewership demand persists; economics depend on title stability and sponsorship depth. We assess esports assets on cash-flow durability, not audience headlines.

Can GreyRadius run our India go-to-market?+

Yes. GTM Execution-as-a-Service covers live-ops calendars, creator programmes, UA management and partnership operations under your direction.
