International Cosmetics Brand - UAE Market Entry & GTM

Market assessment, consumer segmentation and channel strategy for a premium cosmetics launch.

UAE Cosmetics · Market Entry & GTM
Client identity withheld: This case study is based on the supplied GreyRadius case-study deck. The scope and outcomes below are presented as provided; no client name or confidential commercial data is disclosed.
Problem statement

Entering a competitive UAE beauty market with a scalable model.

The international cosmetics brand needed to identify priority consumer segments, product categories and use occasions while assessing competitive positioning, pricing and channel dynamics. The brief was to balance premium brand positioning with market penetration and build a model that could scale beyond the initial launch.

Solution

A Dubai-first GTM model using MCCCI intelligence.

Market, Customer, Competitive and Channel Intelligence connected the commercial decisions.

Market assessment

Evaluated UAE cosmetics market attractiveness, category trends and growth opportunities.

Consumer intelligence

Identified priority cohorts by beauty needs, preferences and willingness to pay.

Competitive benchmarking

Assessed international and regional brands across portfolio, pricing, positioning and promotions.

Channel strategy

Evaluated e-commerce, modern trade, specialty beauty and distributor-led channels.

Phased GTM design

Covered target emirates, channels, portfolio and brand positioning for a practical launch sequence.

From market uncertainty to a replicable launch roadmap.
