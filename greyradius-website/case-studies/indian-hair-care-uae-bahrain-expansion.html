Indian Hair Care Brand - UAE & Bahrain Market Expansion

Market expansion, channel penetration and consumer growth strategy for a hair-care brand moving beyond its traditional customer base.

Indian Hair Care · UAE & Bahrain
Client identity withheld: This case study is based on the supplied GreyRadius case-study deck. The scope and outcomes below are presented as provided; no client name or confidential commercial data is disclosed.
Problem statement

Expanding from ethnic-led distribution to mainstream GCC demand.

The established Indian hair-care brand had sales concentrated among select Indian and ethnic consumer segments and channels in the UAE and Bahrain. It needed to broaden distribution and visibility, prioritise products and channels, and create a scalable approach to mainstream consumer acquisition.

Solution

A targeted expansion strategy for two GCC markets.

Market assessment

Evaluated UAE and Bahrain hair-care markets, category dynamics and growth opportunities.

Consumer intelligence

Identified high-potential consumer segments and hair-care needs.

Competitive benchmarking

Assessed Indian, regional and international brands across products, pricing, positioning and distribution.

Channel strategy

Prioritised modern trade, beauty retail, pharmacies, e-commerce and traditional trade based on market potential.

Portfolio optimisation

Identified hero products and priority SKUs for expansion.

Market expansion

Developed country-specific distributor, channel and activation priorities for UAE and Bahrain.

Impact and results

A route to broader distribution and mainstream acquisition.

  • Enabled increased distribution and visibility across UAE and Bahrain.
  • Expanded reach beyond traditional Indian and ethnic retail channels.
  • Improved channel and SKU-level prioritisation for market investment.
  • Established a structured approach to mainstream consumer acquisition.
  • Created a replicable GCC expansion playbook for further regional growth.
