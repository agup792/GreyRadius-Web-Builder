Daily Dairy Brand – New Product Launch and GTM Strategy

A daily dairy brand needed to launch into one of India's most saturated FMCG categories, then carry that launch into a profitable international expansion – without a validated map of where the whitespace actually was.

300+Interviews and surveys
20%UAE retail outlet coverage increase
4+New SKUs for export market
Yr 1Profitable international expansion

The Situation

A crowded category, dominant regional players, and no validated whitespace.

The client needed to launch a new dairy product into a highly saturated Indian market, dominated by regional players with entrenched price competition. Objectives were clear but unproven: identify whitespace in the mass and premium dairy segments, build differentiated positioning in a cluttered category, design a pricing and distribution strategy for rapid penetration, and create a structured GTM playbook to drive early adoption.

GreyRadius was engaged to answer the whitespace question with evidence, not assumption – and to carry that evidence through to an international expansion that needed to be profitable from Year 1, not Year 3.

The engagement combined category analysis across milk, flavoured milk, curd, paneer, and value-added dairy with direct voice-of-customer research and a structured 7 micro-market scoring exercise in Bengaluru.

What We Did

Four work-streams from category analysis to a national GTM rollout.

1

Market opportunity assessment

Category analysis across milk, flavoured milk, curd, paneer, and value-added dairy – sizing where growth was concentrated and where incumbents were vulnerable.

2

Consumer intelligence and segmentation

Voice-of-customer research across urban families, health-conscious millennials, and price-sensitive households – mapping purchase drivers segment by segment.

3

Portfolio and SKU prioritisation

Identified high-rotation SKUs based on profitability, pricing elasticity, and distribution feasibility – the launch range was built to earn shelf space fast.

4

Brand positioning framework

A functional and emotional value proposition balancing trust, freshness, and aspirational appeal – differentiated from incumbent regional players.

"We knew our product was good. What we didn't know was where the whitespace was, or whether it would travel. GreyRadius answered both questions before we spent a rupee on national rollout."

How We Did It

300+ total interviews and surveys, plus structured cross-functional workshops.

  • 100+ personal interviews: competitor growth and sales interviews, dairy and category experts, distributors and retailers, GT/MT retail surveys, and consumer surveys across working and non-working professionals
  • Structured workshops with business, sales, marketing, and product teams on category entry ambition, pricing guardrails, geographic rollout, distributor onboarding, and trade scheme design
  • 7 high-potential Bengaluru micro-markets scored and mapped with population data and residential coverage
The Outcome

A whitespace-validated launch that carried into a profitable Year 1 export expansion.
